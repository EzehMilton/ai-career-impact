What drives your AI Exposure

What increases AI Exposure

What reduces AI Exposure

  • The work is less repetitive or standardised

  • Trust, persuasion or relationships matter to successful performance

    Keep this step small and note what you learn.

  • Use these results to support your own judgement, not to replace it.
