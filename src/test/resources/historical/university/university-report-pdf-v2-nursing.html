Safeguards

Your own work in assessed modules

In Module 1 and Module 8, AI may be useful for learning and preparation, but these modules strongly depend on demonstrating your own capability. Check your institution's assessment rules before using AI in assessed work.

Professional and client-facing standards

Module 1, Module 3, Module 4 and 4 other modules appear to involve professional, clinical or client-facing standards. Treat AI output as support rather than authority, and verify important conclusions against appropriate course or professional guidance.

Checking AI-generated technical work

When AI helps with calculations, code or technical reasoning in Module 2 and Module 3, check its working yourself: AI output can look correct and still be wrong.

Checking AI-generated claims and sources

When AI summarises reading or suggests claims, quotations or references for Module 4, Module 5, Module 6 and 1 other module, check them against the original sources before relying on them.

Your action plan

The plan below turns these findings into steps for now, the coming months and later study.

Next 30 days

  • Find the course or professional guidance that applies to Module 1, and keep it to hand as the reference you check AI output against.
  • Take one AI-generated summary or claim about your reading for Module 4 and check it against the original source, including any quotations or references.
  • Before your next practical session in Module 8, agree one skill to work on and how you will get feedback on it.
  • Pick the topic in Module 2 you find hardest and work on it without AI first, so you can judge AI explanations of it later.

This term

  • Compare one AI answer about Module 1 with your course or professional guidance, and note where they differ and which you would rely on.
  • Use group, client or patient-facing work in Module 1 to get feedback on how you communicate.
  • In Module 4, form and write down your own interpretation before you consult AI, then compare the two.
  • Ask for feedback on your practical work in Module 8, and act on one point before the next assessment.

Next 6 to 12 months

  • Look for placements, projects or volunteering that add further hands-on experience alongside Module 1 and Module 8.
  • Follow how professional guidance in your field treats AI use, and how it applies to work like Module 1.
  • Collect examples of decisions, interpretations or communication you handled yourself in Module 1, Module 4, Module 6 and 2 other modules, so you can describe your judgement to others.
  • Build a small portfolio of your own work from Module 1 and Module 8, showing your process as well as the result.
