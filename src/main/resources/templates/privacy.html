Your information

What happens to what you tell us

A plain description of how this application currently handles your information.

This is a product information notice, not a legal privacy policy.

It describes what the software does today. It is not legal advice and it is not a formal policy document. A deployment that handles real users' data would need its own policy.

What is stored

  • Your assessment. What you typed, the judgments it produced and the resulting measures are saved, so that your result page keeps working and so that a saved result never silently changes.
  • Your explanation and any external context gathered for that assessment.
  • Purchases. If you buy a full report, we store the purchase, the exact report you bought, and the PDF generated from it.

CV uploads

In the professional assessment you may upload a CV to create a concise professional profile. The file is read in memory, its text is sent to the configured profile-extraction provider, and the file itself and the extracted text are not kept. You review or correct the structured role, summary, responsibilities, skills and experience before continuing. What is saved is the confirmed assessment input, plus a note that it came from an upload, the sanitised file name, its type and when it was parsed.

Before your CV text is sent to our AI provider, we remove email addresses, phone numbers, web links and postcodes. Other details in your CV, including your name, may still be sent.

Payment

Card details are entered on Stripe's own hosted checkout page and are never seen by, or stored in, this application. We keep the payment references Stripe gives us so we can tell that your purchase succeeded. In development mode no payment is taken at all and no card details are ever requested.

External providers

When live integrations are switched on, limited information is sent to external services: what you described goes to the AI judgment and explanation providers, and an occupation or subject reference may be used to look up published statistics. Your result page and your saved assessment are not sent anywhere. In development mode no external provider is contacted at all.

How your result is reached

There are no accounts yet. Your result lives at a long, unguessable web address, and there is no page anywhere that lists assessments. That address is the only way to reach your result, so treat the link as private — anyone you send it to can open it. Proper accounts and sign-in are planned, and this link-based access is a deliberate, temporary limitation rather than the finished design.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or date of birth.
  • There is no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking in this application.
  • We do not sell or share your assessment with anyone.
  • API keys and provider credentials are never stored with your data or shown in the interface.
