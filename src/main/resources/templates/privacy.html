01 / Your assessment

What we save

The application saves the information needed to return to your assessment and, if purchased, your detailed report.

01

Your assessment

What you entered, the AI judgments and the resulting measures. Saving these means an existing assessment does not silently change.

02

Explanations and context

The written explanation and any external context gathered for that assessment.

03

Full-report purchases

If you purchase a report, the purchase record, the exact report you bought and the PDF generated from it.

02 / Purchases

How payments work

Card details are entered on Stripe's hosted checkout page, rather than into this application. The application keeps payment references from Stripe to confirm purchases; it does not receive or store card details.

In the described development mode, payments are simulated and no card details are requested.

03 / Your results

How you access your results

There are currently no user accounts. Each assessment can be reopened using its long, hard-to-guess link; there is no public page listing assessments.

Keep your result link private. Anyone who has the link may be able to open your assessment. Link-only access is a current limitation, not a substitute for all account and security controls.

04 / Other services

When external services are involved

When live integrations are enabled, the application sends relevant information to providers that perform particular tasks. It does not send a result-page link or an entire saved assessment record as part of the described flow.

ServiceWhy it's used and what it receives
AI assessment and explanation

Receives relevant information about the work, course or subjects you described, so it can provide structured judgments and explanations.

Published-data sources

May receive an occupation or subject reference to look up external statistics or factual context.

Stripe

Handles hosted checkout and payment processing when you buy a full report.

According to the application's current description, development mode does not contact external providers. Actual provider identities, their retention practices and any international transfers need to be verified and documented before launch.

05 / Boundaries

What we don't do

  • We do not require your name, email address or date of birth to complete an assessment. Free-text answers may still contain personal information.
  • The application is described as having no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking.
  • We do not sell assessment information. Relevant information may be shared with service providers as described above.
  • API keys and provider credentials are not stored with assessment data or shown in the interface.
