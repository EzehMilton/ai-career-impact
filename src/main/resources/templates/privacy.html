Your information

How we handle your information

A simple explanation of what the application saves, when information is sent to other services and how you can access your results.

At a glance

The essentials

  • You do not need an account, name or email address to complete an assessment.
  • We save your assessment and result so you can return to it later.
  • Stripe handles card payments. We do not see or store your card details.
  • Your result is available through a long private link, so keep that link private.

What we keep

What we save

We save the information needed to show your assessment again and provide a detailed report if you buy one.

Your assessment
What you entered, the structured AI ratings and the scores produced from them.
Your explanation and context
The written explanation and any outside data shown with your result.
Your report purchase
If you buy a report, we save the purchase record, the report and its PDF.

Other services

When information leaves the application

Some features use external services. Only the information relevant to the task is sent as part of the described flow.

AI assessment and explanation

Receives relevant information about the work, course or subjects you described so it can provide structured judgments and explanations.

Published data sources

May receive an occupation or subject reference when the application looks up external statistics or factual context.

Stripe

Handles hosted checkout and payment processing when you buy a detailed report.

Access and payments

How results and purchases work

Your result link

There are no accounts. Each result has its own long private link and results are not listed publicly.

Anyone with the link may be able to open the assessment, so keep it private.

Payments

You pay on Stripe's secure checkout page rather than on this site.

We receive a payment reference to confirm the purchase, but do not see or store your card details.

Boundaries

What we don't do

  • We do not require your name, email address or date of birth to complete an assessment.
  • The application is described as having no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking.
  • We do not sell assessment information.
  • API keys and provider credentials are not stored with assessment data or shown in the interface.

Free-text answers can still contain personal information if you choose to include it.
