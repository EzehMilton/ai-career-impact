How to read your profile

Each score runs from 0 to 10. Scores below 3.5, from 3.5 to below 7, and 7 and above fall into the three bands shown. A higher number means more of what that part of the profile describes, not a better set of subjects.

The parts of your profile are shown side by side and never added into one score. They describe your subject combination, not your ability, your grades or your chances of a particular university place or job.

About your assessment

This is a structured reading of the subjects you listed. It is not an admissions decision, not official careers guidance and not a ranking of subjects. Always check the published entry requirements of any course you are considering.
