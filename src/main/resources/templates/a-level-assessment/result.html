How to understand your A-level ratings

Future Optionality describes how wide a range of plausible next steps your current subject combination appears to keep open, and how widely the skills it builds transfer. Each indicator runs from 0 to 10. It is not a probability.

This is not a prediction of university admission.

A more focused combination is a perfectly reasonable choice: it may point more directly towards fewer destinations. This assessment describes your subjects, not your abilities or worth. Higher ratings are not automatically better for your personal goals.
