What Future Optionality means

Future Optionality describes how wide a range of plausible next steps your current subject combination appears to keep open, and how widely the skills it builds transfer. Each number runs from 0 to 10 and is an indicator, not a probability.

This is not a prediction of university admission.

A more focused combination is a perfectly reasonable choice — it simply points more directly at fewer destinations. It describes your subjects, not you.
