What Future Optionality means

Future Optionality describes how wide a range of plausible next steps your current subject combination appears to keep open, and how widely the skills it builds transfer. It is not a prediction about university admission, not a score of how employable you are, and not a verdict on your choices. A more focused combination is a perfectly reasonable choice — it simply points more directly at fewer destinations.
