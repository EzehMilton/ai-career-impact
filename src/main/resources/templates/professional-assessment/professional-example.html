What a useful completed form can look like

This example shows the level of detail that helps us understand what someone actually does at work. Your answers do not need to use the same wording or be this exact length.

Use this as a guide, not a template

Describe your own work in your own words. Two people with the same job title may do very different things, so the assessment works best when your answers reflect your real day-to-day responsibilities.
