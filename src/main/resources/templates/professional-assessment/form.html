How will AI affect the work you do?

AI affects tasks differently even when people share the same job title. Describe what you actually do so the assessment can look at the nature of your work.

Who this is for, and what you'll need

This is for anyone already working, or with a role they can describe. You will need a few minutes and a sense of your day-to-day responsibilities and the skills you use. You can type everything in, or upload a CV and review the concise professional profile we understand from it.

It describes the characteristics of the work: how repeatable it is, how much it depends on human trust and context, and where AI could help. It will not tell you whether you will lose your job, and no number here is a probability of that happening.
