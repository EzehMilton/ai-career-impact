How will AI affect the work you do?

AI may affect people with the same job title differently. Tell us what you do in your day-to-day work so we can assess which activities AI could change and where your skills may remain valuable.

Before you begin

This assessment is for people who are working or have a role they can describe. You'll need to tell us about your job, day-to-day work, main responsibilities and the skills you use.

The more specific you can be about what you actually do, the more useful your assessment can be. Focus on your real work rather than what someone with your job title might usually do.

Tell us about your work

Focus on what you actually do, rather than just your job title. The prompts and examples below are there to help you give enough detail for a useful assessment.

For example: Software Engineer, Project Manager or Care Assistant.

Describe what you spend your time doing, the decisions you make and the people you work with. For example: “I discuss requirements with clients, develop and review software, help colleagues solve problems and make technical decisions.”

List your main responsibilities, one per line. Include recurring work, responsibilities that require judgement, and anything where you are responsible for an outcome. For example: Review technical designs; Meet clients to discuss requirements; Resolve production issues.

Include technical, practical and people skills, one per line. For example: Problem solving; Communication; Java programming; Team leadership.
