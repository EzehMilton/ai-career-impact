How to understand your course profile

Each measure runs from 0 to 10 and is shown as Low (below 3.5), Moderate (3.5 to below 7) or High (7 and above). A higher number means more of the characteristic being measured, not a better or worse course.

The three measures are shown side by side. They are not added together into one score, and they describe the course, not your abilities, your future career, employment or salary.
