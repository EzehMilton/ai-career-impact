How to understand your ratings

What Future Readiness means

Future Readiness describes how well the course appears to develop capabilities that may remain useful as AI tools become more capable. Each indicator runs from 0 to 10. A higher number means more of the characteristic being measured, not necessarily a better personal outcome.

This is not a guarantee of employment.

The ratings describe the course, not your abilities or future career. They are not university rankings and cannot be compared with results from a different type of assessment.
