University course assessment

How well does this course position you for an AI-shaped labour market?

This looks at the skills a course develops, not at whether AI will take a particular job. There are no right or wrong answers, and nothing here decides anything for you. The more you can say about what the course actually covers, the more useful the result.

Who this is for, and what you'll need

This is for anyone choosing a degree, holding an offer, or already studying one. You will need a few minutes and the course details — a prospectus page or course listing is ideal.

This assesses the course, not you. It says nothing about your intelligence, your ability or your worth, and it is not a measure of how employable you are. It describes what the course appears to teach and how widely those capabilities tend to apply.

Tell us about the course

You do not need any experience of employment. Course pages and prospectuses are a good place to copy details from.

For example: the main subjects, how much is coursework or exams, whether there are labs, studio work, a placement or a final-year project.
