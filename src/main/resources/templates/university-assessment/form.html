Before you begin

This assessment is for anyone considering a degree, holding an offer or already studying. Have your course description or university prospectus handy. You can use it to complete the form below.

We assess what the course teaches, not your personal ability or future employability. You'll receive a free assessment, with an optional detailed report afterwards.

Tell us about your course

You don't need work experience. Use the course page or prospectus to describe the subjects, modules and skills the course covers. If you're unsure about a detail, check the official course listing rather than guessing.

Use the official course title where possible.

Describe what students learn and how they learn it. Include any practical projects, placements, laboratory work or other important activities.

List the main modules or subjects, one per line. You can copy their names from the course page.

Include practical and transferable skills, one per line. Look for learning outcomes on the course page if you need ideas.

If you have career interests, list them one per line. They provide context for your assessment; this field does not generate a career recommendation.

For reference only. We don't rank universities or use league tables in this assessment.

Your assessment is free. You can choose whether to purchase a more detailed PDF report after seeing your results.
