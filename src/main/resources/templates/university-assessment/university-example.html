What a useful completed course form can look like

This example shows the kind of detail that helps us assess what a course actually teaches. Use the official course page or prospectus where possible, and describe your own course rather than copying this example.

Use this as a guide, not a template

Courses with the same title can differ considerably between universities. Your assessment works best when the description, modules and skills reflect the specific course you are considering or studying.

Course or degree title

BSc Computer Science

What does the course cover?

The course covers programming, software engineering, databases, algorithms, computer systems, artificial intelligence and data analysis. Students learn through lectures, practical laboratory sessions, individual programming assignments and group software projects. The course also includes a final-year project where students research, design and build a substantial technical solution.
