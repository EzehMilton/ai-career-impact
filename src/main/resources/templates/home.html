Think clearly about AI and your future

AI is changing how people work and what they study. This is a calm, structured way to look at how your work, your course or your subject choices might fit a labour market that AI is reshaping.

It will not tell you what will happen to you. Nobody can. What it will do is describe the characteristics of what you have told us about, and explain what those characteristics tend to mean.

Being straight with you

Every number here is an indicator, not a probability and not a prediction. A result is never a guarantee of employment, university admission or career success, and a low number is not a verdict on you or your choices.

The three assessments deliberately measure different things, so their numbers are not comparable with each other. Where we can point at external facts — official statistics, course information — we show them separately, with their source, so you can tell the difference between a fact and our assessment.
