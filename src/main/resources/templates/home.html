How results are produced

Structured reasoning, not a single AI verdict.

Your scores come from a fixed method, not a chatbot’s opinion, so you can see exactly why you got them. They’re a guide to help you decide, and cannot predict your individual future or guarantee particular outcomes.

  • AI answers specific questions

    AI rates what you told us against a fixed set of clear questions. It doesn’t decide your overall result. One big AI judgement can vary and is hard to explain, so we keep its job small and checkable.

  • Fixed rules set your scores

    The same published rules turn those ratings into your scores, every time. The written explanation is added afterwards and can’t change them.

  • What we won't do

    We won’t predict whether you’ll lose your job or get a place, rank courses or universities, make up statistics, or tell you to change your subjects or career.

Common questions

Anything else you’d like to know?

Will it tell me whether I'll lose my job?

No. Nobody can predict that for an individual. We show how much of your work AI could change, where your human skills still matter, and how your role could adapt, so you can plan ahead.

Is this just a chatbot's opinion?

No. AI answers specific questions, and fixed rules turn those answers into your scores. See exactly how it works.

Do I need an account?

No, and we don't ask for your name or email. Each result is saved at a long, private link, so bookmark it to come back later. Anyone with the link can open it, so don't share it publicly.

Can I compare courses or subject combinations?

Yes. Run the assessment again with a different course or combination. Each one is saved separately at its own link. Scores from different assessment types (say, a university score and an A-level score) measure different things and shouldn't be compared.

How do I pay for a detailed report?

It's a one-off payment, made after you've seen your free result, through Stripe's secure checkout. We never see your card details, and there's no subscription.

What’s in the detailed report?

Practical next steps based on your result, the skills to build first, a fuller explanation of every measure and a PDF to keep. Prices are £4.99 for professional, £2.99 for university and £1.99 for A-level. You decide after seeing your free result.
