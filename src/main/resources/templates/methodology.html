How this works

How your assessment works

The assessment separates AI judgement from the rules that produce your scores. This page explains each part of the process, what the scores mean and the limits we deliberately place on the system.

Our process

Four steps, with different responsibilities

AI does not simply look at your information and decide an overall score. The assessment is deliberately split into separate stages.

  1. 01

    AI makes structured judgments

    An AI model answers a fixed set of small, specific questions about what you described, such as how repeatable a type of work is or how much analytical reasoning a subject combination develops. Each question has a defined scale with its levels written in advance. The model does not produce the final assessment scores.

  2. 02

    Versioned rules produce the scores

    Fixed rules in the application combine those judgments using defined weights and thresholds. Under the same methodology version, the same judgments produce the same scores and bands.

  3. 03

    AI explains the result afterwards

    After the scores have been decided, AI is used again to turn the result into readable prose. It receives the result; it does not calculate it. If an explanation conflicts with the assessment, we reject the explanation rather than changing the scores.

  4. 04

    External evidence stays separate

    Where we have a reliable outside source, such as published labour-market data, we may show it alongside the result with its source and date. External evidence provides context and does not change your scores.

Different questions, different measures

The three assessments are not comparable

Professional, University and A-level assessments answer different questions, so their measures and bands mean different things.

Professional

AI Exposure

How much your work contains activities that AI could perform, support or significantly change. It is not a probability of job loss.

University

Future Readiness

How well a course develops capabilities that may remain valuable as AI changes work and professional practice. It is not a guarantee of employability.

A-level

Future Optionality

How broadly a subject combination may keep future education and career options open. It is not a guarantee of university admission or career success.

Each assessment uses its own judgments, rules and bands. A score in one assessment cannot be compared with the same number in another, and the application deliberately does not produce one overall “AI score”.

Scoring policy

How your scores are worked out

The rules that combine the judgments are our own provisional product policy: a transparent starting point, not validated educational or economic science and not the view of any employer, university or admissions body.

Each methodology version is recorded with the assessment. If the rules change, new assessments can use a new version without changing old results.

Stored result

Your result is a snapshot

When an assessment is completed, its inputs, judgments, scores and methodology version are stored together. Reopening the result shows the saved assessment rather than running it again or silently recalculating it under newer rules.

Free and detailed

Paying adds depth, not a better score

The free assessment and the detailed report use the same underlying result. The free version gives you the measures, the main factors behind them and a short interpretation. The detailed report adds fuller explanations, practical next steps, skills to prioritise, external context where available and a downloadable PDF.

Our boundaries

What we deliberately do not do

  • We do not predict whether you will lose a job, receive an offer or succeed in a career.
  • We do not rank subjects, courses, universities or employers.
  • We do not present external statistics as fact without a source.
  • We do not invent university entry requirements. Course requirements should be checked with the university or course provider.
  • We do not tell you to change your subjects, abandon a course or leave a career.
